# Known Limitations - zregex

Split in F7c-5 into two documents:

- **[LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md):** what zregex does and doesn't do today: the
  `UnsupportedFeature` rule and every form it covers, the known divergences from V8,
  lookbehind, `v` with `i`, the limits and the notes on the API.
- **[HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md):** the record of each phase (F1 to F7c): what it changed,
  measured and fixed.

This file stays because comments in the sources and scripts cite it; a reference to a
section of "KNOWN_LIMITATIONS" is in one of the two.
